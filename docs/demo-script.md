# Build Your Tank — demo script

Your first little world · 预计 3:55 · 英文旁白 / 中文录制说明

时间为镜头规划，可根据实际语速调整。点击 Next step 仅记录已阅读，不验证任务是否实际完成；购买和照料需要手动操作。

## 开场 (0:00–0:15)

Build Your Tank makes aquarium planning approachable. In this demo, I will build a habitat, learn about its inhabitants, and connect everyday care with the health of a small ecosystem.

## 1. Meet your workspace (0:15–0:27)

页面 / 操作：My tanks

新手指引：One place for your tanks, supplies and daily care.

- Look at the starter tank and the collection above it. Each tank keeps its own setup and care history.
- Use the four navigation tabs to explore. Coins and care supplies are shared by all your tanks.

英文旁白：

A starter tank makes the workspace immediately explorable. My tanks, inspiration, the store, and the learning library connect building with learning. Coins and care supplies are shared across my collection.

录制说明：

从首页点击 Build your first tank，输入昵称进入。展示欢迎指引并开始，然后停留在 My tanks。录制前使用独立浏览器配置，避免影响已有鱼缸。

## 2. Find a starting idea (0:27–0:43)

页面 / 操作：Explore Tank idea

新手指引：Explore an example before making your own choices.

- Filter Tank idea by style and open an example to inspect its fish, plants and water status.
- Observe tank opens a closer view. Make my own copy buys a separate tank if you have enough coins; copying is optional.

英文旁白：

Tank idea gives beginners a starting point. I can inspect the inhabitants and equipment of an example before building. Purchasing a separate copy is also possible, while preserving my existing tanks.

录制说明：

打开一个示例缸，展示物种和配置，关闭弹窗即可。主线演示自行搭建，不购买示例副本，以保持预算和操作连贯。示例不是实时社区用户。

## 3. Create a tank of your own (0:43–1:03)

页面 / 操作：Open New tank

新手指引：Give your new world a name and a place in the room.

- Choose New tank, enter a name, and compare 60cm, 120cm and 150cm against the same furniture.
- Choose a size and glass finish within your budget, then create the tank. Switch collection cards to return to another tank.
- You can rename the selected tank later using the name field above its room view.

英文旁白：

I will create Quiet Garden. Comparing three sizes against the same furniture makes space a visible design decision. Each tank is independent, so switching between designs preserves the choices I have already made.

录制说明：

新建 Quiet Garden，依次点选尺寸展示比例，最终选 60cm 和 Regular glass，点击创建。切换旧缸再切回新缸，展示隔离保存。

## 4. Build the habitat first (1:03–1:21)

页面 / 操作：Open Edit setup

新手指引：Choose the space, substrate, glass and filtration.

- Open Edit setup to adjust tank size, glass finish, substrate and filtration.
- Upgrades spend the price difference and trade-ins refund it. The room view and learning status respond to your setup.

英文旁白：

Before adding inhabitants, I choose their habitat: the tank, glass, substrate, and filter. Upgrades spend the difference in demo coins, and trade-ins refund it, making equipment and budget choices easy to explore.

录制说明：

打开 Edit setup，选择 ADA Aqua Soil Amazonia，保留海绵过滤。展示可修改的尺寸、玻璃和过滤字段。不要为了镜头连续购买昂贵升级。

## 5. Learn before you choose (1:21–1:39)

页面 / 操作：Explore Learn

新手指引：Understand an inhabitant before bringing it home.

- Search Learn for Betta, open the Bettas group, then open the Betta encyclopedia entry.
- Read habitat, temperament, tankmate and care information. The library also covers plants, substrates, equipment and supplies.
- Browse categories and fish groups to compare related species, then return to the library or keep an entry link for later.

英文旁白：

Learn connects a visual preference with an informed choice. Searching for a betta opens information about its habitat, temperament, and care needs. The library also explains plants, equipment, and supplies.

录制说明：

搜索 Betta，进入 Bettas 群组，展示群组介绍，再打开 Betta 词条查看栖息地与同缸兼容信息。说明本次展示单只斗鱼的设计选择，避免把默认示例缸视为真实饲养建议。

## 6. Bring your world to life (1:39–2:01)

页面 / 操作：Visit Fish store

新手指引：Shop for the selected tank, one addition at a time.

- Check Shopping for before buying. Search or filter fish, open species details, and add a suitable inhabitant.
- Switch to Plants to add greenery. Fish and plants go directly into the selected tank; removing one refunds its demo price.

英文旁白：

In Fish store, I check which tank I am shopping for. Species details connect to the library. I will add one betta and two plants to Quiet Garden, then see those choices appear in my tank.

录制说明：

确认 Shopping for 为 Quiet Garden。搜索 Betta，只添加 1 只；清空搜索，切 Plants，添加 Anubias Kirin 与 Rotala 各 1。回到 My tanks 展示结果。

## 7. Take a closer look (2:01–2:13)

页面 / 操作：Observe tank

新手指引：Move from a room view to the details of your aquarium.

- Choose Observe tank on the room view to open the aquarium close-up.
- Use zoom, scroll or swipe to look around. Reset view returns to 100%; zoom changes your view, not the tank size.

英文旁白：

Observe tank moves from the room to a close-up. I can zoom and look around, then reset the view without changing the aquarium itself.

录制说明：

打开 Observe tank，放大到 150%，稍作横向滚动，再 Reset view，关闭。鱼的移动是展示动画。

## 8. Understand the water (2:13–2:29)

页面 / 操作：Find water settings

新手指引：Explore how a habitat is more than what you can see.

- Read the inhabitants, pH and space status, then find Water & nutrients beside the tank.
- Explore pH, temperature, hardness and nutrients. Watch the educational warnings and return to your original values after experimenting.

英文旁白：

Water settings make less visible conditions readable. Changing temperature can trigger an educational warning. These are simplified demo settings, not live measurements; hardness is saved as a reference rather than modeled chemically.

录制说明：

展示 Water & nutrients，将温度从 25 改为 32，展示警告，再恢复 25。不要说成传感器监测、真实水质检测或物种精确预测。

## 9. Prepare for daily care (2:29–2:41)

页面 / 操作：Open My bag

新手指引：Know where your coins and supplies go.

- Open My bag to see shared food portions, water refills and fertilizer doses.
- Daily check-in grants 20 coins once per local date. In Fish store, Care supplies buys packs into your shared bag, not into one tank.

英文旁白：

My bag keeps supplies together across tanks. Daily check-in earns demo coins, and care supplies can be restocked in the store for future routines.

录制说明：

展示 My bag 后关闭，领取 Daily check-in 的 20 coins（已领取则展示状态）。进入 Fish store → Care supplies 购买一包食物，展示库存增加。

## 10. Practice a small daily routine (2:41–3:01)

页面 / 操作：Find daily care

新手指引：Feed, refresh the water and care for your plants.

- Feed fish uses one food portion and earns 5 coins. Change water uses one refill and earns 8 coins.
- Care for plants uses one fertilizer dose and increases the nutrient index. Each care action is available once per tank per local day.

英文旁白：

Feeding and water care consume supplies and reward attention with demo coins. Fertilizer changes the nutrient index. Each task records completion for this tank and day, encouraging a small, repeatable routine.

录制说明：

确保选中 Quiet Garden，依次 Feed fish、Change water、Care for plants。每个动画停留约 2 秒，展示完成状态和库存扣减。重录请使用新的演示鱼缸，勿删除已有作品。

## 11. Look ahead and reflect (3:01–3:15)

页面 / 操作：Open 30-day preview

新手指引：Use the 30-day estimate to review your choices.

- Open 30 days later to review the estimated water balance, greenery and available space.
- Treat the result as a simplified learning prompt assuming regular care. Previewing does not advance time or change the saved tank.

英文旁白：

The thirty-day preview brings these choices together in an outlook for water balance, greenery, and space. It is a simplified learning estimate, inviting me to review the habitat and consider improvements.

录制说明：

打开 30 days later，展示三个结果。它不会真的播放 30 天成长，也不会修改存档；旁白明确为简化学习估计。

## 12. Keep it and pass it on (3:15–3:35)

页面 / 操作：Open Share tank

新手指引：Save your work, share a snapshot and keep exploring.

- Your workspace saves automatically in this browser; Save tank confirms a local save.
- Share tank creates a read-only snapshot link. A viewer can purchase a separate copy. In my gallery shows a tank in this browser’s Tank idea gallery; it does not publish online.

英文旁白：

My work saves in this browser. Share tank creates a read-only snapshot that someone can inspect and copy separately. I can revisit the guide, or edit its order, instructions, and recording narration as the project develops.

录制说明：

先点 Save tank，再打开 Share tank 复制链接；可在另一个标签展示只读快照。返回展示 In my gallery 开关和指引编辑入口，结束于完成页。正式录制使用可访问的托管地址，本地链接无法供评审直接访问。

## 结尾 (3:35–3:55)

The goal is to connect creative choices with informed care, so a beginner can understand the world they are building. This working prototype saves locally and shares snapshots. Cloud accounts and a live community are future steps; the water and growth models are simplified educational estimates.
